Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

IQ Test Lab is built to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Your test answers and results. These are stored only in your browser (local storage) on your own device so you can resume the test and view your report. They are not sent to our servers. A finished report stays available for 90 days, and a short history of past scores (date, estimate and skill percentages, up to ten results) is kept so you can see your progress. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.
  • No account, no email. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details to take the test or see your report.
  • Basic visit statistics. We may use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. This does not include your test answers.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider processes standard technical data such as IP address to deliver and protect the site.

Sharing

If you use “Share result image”, the image is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Your choices

You can delete your saved results at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for this website.
