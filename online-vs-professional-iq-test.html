Online vs professional IQ test: which should you take?

Updated November 12, 2026

Online IQ tests are quick and free, but they are not the same as a test given by a psychologist. Here is how they differ, how accurate each is and how to spot a misleading site.

The short answer

Use an online test for curiosity, self-insight and practice. Use a professional test when the result will be used for an educational, clinical, workplace or high-IQ society decision. An online estimate cannot replace a supervised assessment.

When an online test is enough

  • You are curious about your reasoning skills or want a baseline.
  • You want to see your relative strengths across pattern, number, verbal and logical reasoning.
  • You want practice before a supervised test.

When you need a professional test

  • A school, doctor or employer asks for an assessment.
  • You suspect a learning difference or attention difficulty.
  • You want to apply to a high-IQ society such as Mensa.
  • You need a score you can document.

Red flags in online IQ tests

  • Payment before the score: you are asked for card details to see a result, especially a “trial” that can renew into a subscription.
  • Always flattering scores: inflated results encourage sharing and purchases, but they are not honest.
  • No methodology: no explanation of how the score is calculated or what it is based on.
  • False precision: a single exact number with no margin of error.
  • Genius promises: claims that five questions can reveal your genius level.

Online vs professional IQ test FAQ

Are online IQ tests accurate?

Short online IQ tests give a rough estimate. They usually lack large, documented norm groups, are not age-adjusted and are taken in uncontrolled conditions, so the margin of error is wide. They are fine for curiosity, not for official decisions.

Can an online IQ test qualify me for Mensa?

No. Mensa requires a supervised test from an approved source, with a score at or above the 98th percentile. Online practice tests cannot qualify you.

How much does a professional IQ test cost?

Prices vary widely by country, provider and the type of assessment, from a modest fee for some supervised group tests to a substantial professional fee for a full clinical assessment. Ask the provider before booking.
